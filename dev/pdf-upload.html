HOW TO UPLOAD A SAMPLE PDF

1️⃣

Get a PDF

Any PDF works — a magazine, brochure, or multi-page document. For testing, try a free magazine PDF from issuu.com or any local file.

2️⃣

Drop or Browse

Drag the PDF onto the drop zone above, or click "Browse File" to select it. The file stays local — nothing is uploaded to any server.

3️⃣

Click Extract

Hit "Extract Pages". Each page is converted to a JPEG image right in your browser using PDF.js. Previews appear on the right in real time.

4️⃣

Open Reader

Click "Open Reader" — your PDF pages load instantly into the full magazine reader with page-flip animations, zoom, and sound.
